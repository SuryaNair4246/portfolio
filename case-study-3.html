Agentic AI IVR for Elevance Health

Company Overview

Elevance Health, Inc. is a leading American managed healthcare and health benefits company headquartered in Indianapolis, Indiana. Formerly known as Anthem, Inc., the company rebranded in 2022 to better reflect its mission to elevate and advance health beyond traditional insurance coverage. It’s among the largest healthcare companies in the world by revenue and a component of the S&P 500.

Challenges

The challenge was to design and develop a agentic IVR that was highly regulated, contextual and engaging, leveraging cutting-edge AI technology. The solution needed to meet several specific goals:

  • Designing agentic behavior that can take initiative while staying fully compliant.
  • Ensuring the AI’s reasoning and language is 100% accurate and understandable. 
  • Defining when to act autonomously vs. when to escalate to a human agent.
  • Ensuring ASR (speech recognition) and NLU (intent extraction) accurately capture member intent in real-time.

Solution

  • Voice-first UX design: Clarity over cleverness – simple, direct prompts.
  • Intent classification with confidence thresholds.
  • Training the LLM: The chatbot was fine-tuned with domain-specific knowledge using RAG sources.
  • Guardrails in prompt design: Explicitly instruct AI on boundaries.
  • Continuous auditing: Monitor AI responses for unsafe or misleading outputs.

Results

  • Higher CSAT/NPS scores for IVR interactions
  • Shorter average call time (AHT)
  • Controlled autonomous decisions with consistent escalation when uncertain
  • Lower call abandonment rates
